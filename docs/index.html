HUMANCOIN · HUMAN INTENT

HumanCOIN — intent before action

HumanCOIN is building a 12,500-hour first-person manipulation corpus around the human intent pathway: gaze before the hand, muscle load before contact, and cortical activity before the motor command. Its three nested tiers capture proxies for attention (L1), exertion (L2), and cognition (L3) in a shared time and coordinate frame.
